Recommended Reading

  • Nancy Duarte, Resonate
    Best for narrative structure and persuasive storytelling. Useful for helping students frame the problem, explain why it matters, and end with a clear recommendation.
  • Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic, Storytelling with Data
    Best for visual communication of results. Especially useful for presenting charts, fairness metrics, subgroup comparisons, and model diagnostics clearly.
  • Edward Tufte, The Visual Display of Quantitative Information
    Best for rigorous and honest presentation of quantitative evidence. Helps students avoid misleading visuals and present audit results with integrity.
