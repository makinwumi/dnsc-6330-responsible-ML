Potential Questions for In-Class Quiz

Short-answer (7-10 minutes each). Justify claims.

Quiz 1: Distribution Drift

A model trained on historical housing data shows PSI < 0.05 for all individual features, but MMD between train and test is high. Is this evidence of distributional stability or instability? Explain what this reveals about feature interactions and why univariate tests may be insufficient.

Quiz 2: Generalization and Overfitting

A model achieves Train AUC = 0.92 and Test AUC = 0.74. However, PSI and KS tests indicate minimal distribution drift. What is the most likely explanation for this performance gap? Discuss whether this is due to overfitting, data leakage, or concept shift, and justify your answer.

Quiz 3: Spurious Correlations

A counterfactual test shows that swapping race (African-American ↔ Caucasian) changes predicted probabilities by an average of 0.12, holding all other features constant. Does this constitute evidence of algorithmic bias? Explain the distinction between correlation vs. causal dependence and proxy variables vs. direct use of protected attributes.

Quiz 4: Robustness and Stress Testing

After increasing priors_count by +5 in a stress test, mean predicted probability increases significantly and the classification rate shifts from 40% to 65% high-risk. What does this suggest about the model’s robustness? Would you characterize this as expected sensitivity or instability? Justify your answer.

Quiz 5: Slice-Based Evaluation and Risk

A model has overall accuracy of 85%, but FNR for one subgroup is 45%, compared to 12% overall. Is this primarily a performance issue, a fairness issue, or a deployment risk issue? Explain your answer and discuss what action should be taken before deployment.
